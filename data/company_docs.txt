Company Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of receiving the product.

Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

Refunds are normally processed within 5 business days after the returned product is inspected.

Digital products are not eligible for refunds.

Premium customers can request refunds within 14 days.

Order Cancellation Policy

Customers can cancel an order before it is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is not possible.

Customers can contact support for exceptional cases.

Shipping Policy

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

Express delivery usually takes 1 to 2 business days.

Orders above ₹5000 receive free standard shipping.

Security Policy

Passwords are stored using secure hashing.

JWT is used for authentication between the frontend and backend.

Access tokens expire after a configured period.

Customer Data Policy

Customer personal information must not be exposed to unauthorized users.

Only authorized employees can access sensitive customer information.
